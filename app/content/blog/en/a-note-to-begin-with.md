## Start with something small

A useful note can begin with a problem you encountered, a detail you noticed, or an idea you want to explore. Explain the context before you reach for the solution.

> Leave enough room for the reader to follow your thinking.

## Show the work

A short example often says more than a long list of technologies. This code block is here to demonstrate how technical writing will look.

```js
const idea = {
  question: 'What could be simpler?',
  nextStep: 'Make a small experiment',
};
console.log(idea.nextStep);
```
